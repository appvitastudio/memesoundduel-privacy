Privacy Policy — Meme Sound Duel: Voice Battle

Developer: AppVita Studio · Last updated: 2026-11-17

Microphone and voice

The game uses the microphone only while a red "REC — microphone ON" badge is visible, after you press RECORD in a voice challenge. The sound is analysed on your device to compute a similarity score (rhythm, melody, duration, loudness shape). Recordings are not uploaded, not sent to us or to any third party, not used for speech recognition and not used to identify you. No voice profile is created.

By default each recording is deleted from memory right after scoring. If you turn on "Local replay" for a match, the recordings of that match stay in the phone's memory until you delete them, leave the match or the match ends. They are never saved to storage and never backed up.

You can play without the microphone (No Mic Party) and refuse or revoke the permission at any time in Android settings.

Data stored on your device

Settings, player names you type, avatar, statistics, records, campaign progress and audio-latency calibration are stored locally on your device only. You can erase them in Settings → Delete all local data, or by uninstalling the app.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version shows ads through Google AdMob. Google may collect and process device identifiers (such as the advertising ID), IP address, approximate location derived from it, and ad-interaction data to deliver, measure and, where you consent, personalize ads. Where required (e.g. EEA/UK), you are asked for consent through Google's consent form and can change it later in Settings → Ad privacy options. See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. AdMob never receives your recordings.

Sharing

Only if you tap "Share card", an image with player names, avatars and scores (no audio) is passed to the Android share sheet; you choose where to send it.

Children

The game is not directed to children under 13.
